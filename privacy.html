Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

FebScan ("the app") is a free document scanner for Android made by Adrian Rachman. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your scans stay on your phone, and the app has no servers of its own.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos, scans, PDFs, signatures and text you create in the app are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Text recognition (OCR), edge detection and image filters run on your device. The document scanner and text recognition are provided by Google Play services (ML Kit) and process images on-device.
  • Files you choose to Save, Share or Print go only where you send them.

Google Drive backup (optional)

  • If you choose “Sign in with Google”, the app asks for the drive.file permission. This lets FebScan create files in your own Google Drive and see only the files FebScan created. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • The app uses this permission only to upload PDFs and backup copies of your documents to a “FebScan” folder in your Drive, and to list or download those files when you restore them.
  • The app reads your Google account's name and email address (via the Drive API) only to show which account is connected.
  • Your Google data is sent directly between your phone and Google. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising.
  • You can sign out in the app's Settings at any time, and remove FebScan's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

FebScan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What FebScan does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting to the developer.
  • No accounts on any FebScan server (there are none).
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Deleting your data

Delete documents in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone. Delete backups by removing the “FebScan” folder from your Google Drive.

Children

FebScan is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
