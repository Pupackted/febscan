FebScan ✨

A cozy, free document scanner for Android

No adsNo watermark No limitsNo account needed

What it does

  • Scan papers, ID cards, passports and books with automatic edge detection.
  • Clean up scans with filters (Magic, Paper, B&W…) and remove shadows.
  • Make PDFs or images to share, save or print. Add a password or a signature.
  • Read the text in your scans on-device (OCR) and search it.
  • Optionally back up your scans to your own Google Drive.

Your privacy

Your scans stay on your phone. FebScan only talks to the internet if you turn on Google Drive backup, and then only to put your scans into your own Drive. Read the privacy policy.
